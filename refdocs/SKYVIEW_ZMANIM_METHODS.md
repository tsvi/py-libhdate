This document summarizes the comprehensive zmanim calculation methods documented by the
Chazon Shamayim team, which is widely used in Israel for producing zmanim calendars.

---

## Shitat HaAshkenazim (Ashkenazi Method)

This is the prevalent method in Israel, largely based on the customs calculated by
**Rabbi Yechiel Michel Tukachinsky** (הגרי"מ טוקצינסקי) in his calendars.

### Alot HaShachar (Dawn)

| Variant | Calculation | Degrees | Notes |
|---------|-------------|---------|-------|
| Alot I (stringent) | 90 minutes before sunrise converted to degrees | 19.75° | Use only for chumra (stringency), not leniency |
| Alot II (standard) | 72 minutes before sunrise converted to degrees | 16.013° | Can rely on for both chumra and kula |

### Misheyakir (Time for Tallit/Tefillin)

| Location | Degrees | Minutes Before Sunrise |
|----------|---------|------------------------|
| Standard | 11.5° | ~50 minutes |
| Modi'in Illit | 10.5° | Per local rabbinic guidance |
| Zikhron Ya'akov | 10.5° | Per local rabbinic guidance |

### Sunrise (Zricha)

Two options available:
- **Mishor (Sea Level)**: Standard calculation at 0.833° below horizon
- **Visible Sunrise**: Based on ChaiTables (לוחות חי - ביכורי יוסף) which accounts for
  terrain and actual horizon obstruction

**Note**: In most of Israel, the eastern horizon is blocked by mountains, which offsets
the altitude adjustment. Therefore, sea-level sunrise is used as standard.

### Sha'ot Zmaniyot (Proportional Hours)

| Method | Day Definition | Used For |
|--------|----------------|----------|
| MGA | Alot I to Tzait (90 min in degrees) | Zman Shema/Tfilla (chumra) |
| GRA | Sunrise to Sunset | Zman Shema/Tfilla (standard) |

**Important**: For MGA calculation, Tzait is calculated at 90 minutes in degrees after
sunset (symmetrical with Alot I).

### Chatzot (Midday)

Calculated when the sun is at its peak (transit). Note: This differs by ~10 seconds
from the midpoint between sunrise and sunset due to the sun's orbital motion.

### Mincha Gedola

- **Summer (long days)**: 30 zmaniyot minutes after Chatzot (GRA hours)
- **Winter**: 30 fixed minutes after Chatzot

### End of Musaf (Sof Zman Musaf)

End of 7th hour = 1 zmaniyot hour after Chatzot (GRA hours)

### Mincha Ketana

9.5 zmaniyot hours after sunrise (GRA hours)

### Plag HaMincha

1.25 zmaniyot hours before sunset (GRA hours)

### Sunset (Shkia)

| Location Type | Altitude Adjustment |
|---------------|---------------------|
| High locations (like Jerusalem) | Yes - uses highest point elevation |
| Bnei Brak | No - established custom |
| Most Israeli cities | Depends on western horizon |

### Tzait HaKochavim (Nightfall)

| Purpose | Time/Degrees | Notes |
|---------|--------------|-------|
| Daily (Arvit) | 18-35 fixed minutes | Varies by community |
| Shabbat | 8.5° below horizon | Standard for most Ashkenazim |
| Chazon Ish | 9.28° (~40 minutes) | Stricter interpretation |
| Rabbeinu Tam | 72 fixed minutes | After shkia with altitude |

### Fast Days

| Fast Type | Start | End |
|-----------|-------|-----|
| D'Rabbanan (minor) | Alot I (90 min in degrees) | 6.45° (25-30 minutes after shkia) |
| Yom Kippur | Candle lighting time | Same as Motzei Shabbat |
| Tisha B'Av | Shkia on Erev | Same as minor fasts |

### Candle Lighting Customs by Location

| Location | Minutes Before Shkia |
|----------|---------------------|
| Jerusalem | 40 |
| Beitar, Beit Shemesh, Emanuel, Tel Tzion | 40 |
| Petach Tikva, Metula | 40 |
| Tel Aviv, Bnei Brak area | 22 |
| Rehovot, Ashdod, Kiryat Gat | 22 |
| Northern Israel (Haifa and north) | 30 |
| Kiryat Shemona | 24 |
| Elad, Modi'in Illit, Netivot, Ofakim | 30 |
| Europe, USA | 18 |
| England | 15 |
| Default worldwide | 20 |

## Shitat HaSfaradim (Sephardic Method per Rav Ovadia Yosef)

The prevalent method among Sephardic/Mizrachi communities.

### Alot HaShachar

**72 zmaniyot minutes** before sunrise (with altitude adjustment)
- Zmaniyot minutes calculated using GRA hours (sunrise to sunset)
- Only one alot time (no stringent/lenient variants)

### Misheyakir

**66 zmaniyot minutes** before sunrise with altitude
- 6 zmaniyot minutes after Alot
- For sunrise minyan worshippers: 60 zmaniyot minutes in summer, 50 in winter

### Sunrise

**Visible sunrise** based on ChaiTables (terrain-adjusted) is standard

### Sha'ot Zmaniyot for MGA

- From Alot (72 zmaniyot) to Rabbeinu Tam (symmetrical)
- Ensures Chatzot remains at midday

### Sha'ot Zmaniyot for GRA

From visible sunrise (with altitude) to sunset (with altitude)

### Mincha Gedola

**30 fixed minutes** after Chatzot (year-round)

### Plag HaMincha

1.25 GRA zmaniyot hours before **Tzait** (13.5 zmaniyot minutes)

### Sunset

**Always with altitude adjustment** at all locations

### Tzait HaKochavim (Daily)

**13.5 zmaniyot minutes** after sunset (per Geonim)

### Candle Lighting

Per Rav Ovadia: 20 fixed minutes everywhere
In practice: Often follows local Ashkenazi custom

### Motzei Shabbat

**30 fixed minutes** after sunset (with altitude)

### Rabbeinu Tam

**72 zmaniyot minutes** after sunset (with altitude)

### End of Minor Fasts

**20 fixed minutes** after sunset

## Rabbi Y.M. Tukachinsky's Original Tables

These are the exact times as published in his calendars (Jerusalem only):

| Zman | Method |
|------|--------|
| Alot HaShachar | 90 minutes in degrees |
| Sunrise | Visible |
| Zman Shema MGA | Per above alot |
| Mincha Gedola | Chumra (zmaniyot when applicable) |
| Sunset | With altitude |
| Motzei Shabbat | 8.5° |

**Note**: These tables were calculated decades ago. Due to solar precession, times
may have shifted slightly. Modern recalculation using his methodology is recommended.

## Implementation Notes for py-libhdate

### Mapping to ZmanimConfig

```python
# Ashkenazi standard (GRA-based)
ZmanimConfig(
    calculation_method=CalculationMethod.GRA,
    alot_hashachar=ZmanMethod(degrees=16.013),  # 72 min equivalent
    misheyakir=ZmanMethod(degrees=11.5),
    tzait_hakochavim=ZmanMethod(degrees=8.5),
    tzait_fast_day=ZmanMethod(degrees=6.45),
)

# Ashkenazi stringent (MGA-based)
ZmanimConfig(
    calculation_method=CalculationMethod.MGA,
    alot_hashachar=ZmanMethod(degrees=19.75),  # 90 min equivalent
    misheyakir=ZmanMethod(degrees=11.5),
    tzait_hakochavim=ZmanMethod(degrees=8.5),
)

# Sephardic (Rav Ovadia)
# Note: Requires zmaniyot minutes support
ZmanimConfig(
    calculation_method=CalculationMethod.GRA,
    use_elevation=True,  # Always use altitude
    candle_lighting=ZmanMethod(minutes=20),
    tzait_shabbat=ZmanMethod(minutes=30),
)
```

### Features Not Yet Supported

1. **Zmaniyot minutes** - Variable minutes based on day length
2. **Visible sunrise** from ChaiTables - Terrain-adjusted
3. **Location-specific candle lighting** - Per-city customs
4. **Symmetric MGA** - Alot to matching Tzait
